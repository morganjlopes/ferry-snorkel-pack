How it works

Three steps. One of them is snorkeling.

We built Ferry Snorkel Pack around the ferry schedule, so the gear part of your day takes less time than buying a coffee.

  1. Reserve online

    Pick your date, your dock, and your fin sizes. It takes two minutes, and you can book up to 6 a.m. on the day you sail.

  2. Grab it at the dock

    Your pack sits in a numbered locker by the boarding gate. Tap in your pickup code, lift out the bag, and walk onto the ferry.

  3. Drop it on return

    Step off the last ferry and drop the whole bag in the orange return bin. Sandy and wet is fine. We take it from there.

FAQ

Good questions.

The short answers to what people ask most before their first pack.

How do I pick the right fin size?

Choose by US shoe size when you reserve. Our fins have adjustable heel straps, so each size covers a range. If you are between sizes, go down. Masks are one adjustable size for adults and one for kids, and we pack the right one based on your fin size.

How is the gear cleaned?

Every mask, snorkel, and fin set is washed, soaked in a marine-safe disinfectant, rinsed, and air dried. Snorkel mouthpieces are replaced after each use, not just cleaned. Then the pack is sealed with a dated tag. If the seal is broken when you open your locker, text the number on the locker and we will swap it.

What if my ferry back is late?

No late fees for late ferries. Return bins stay open until after the last scheduled arrival at each dock. If you miss the bin hours entirely, drop the pack by 10 a.m. the next morning and you are fine.

What if I lose or break something?

It happens. Normal wear, scratches, and a lost sunscreen cap are on us. For lost gear we charge a flat replacement fee: $12 for a snorkel, $18 for a mask, $25 for a pair of fins. Never more than $45 per snorkeler.

Can I return my pack at a different dock?

Yes. Any orange Ferry Snorkel Pack bin at any of our three docks works. The tag on the bag tells us it is yours.

What if the weather turns or the ferry is cancelled?

If your sailing is cancelled, your reservation is cancelled with it and you owe nothing. You can also cancel or move your date yourself for free until 6 a.m. on the day.
